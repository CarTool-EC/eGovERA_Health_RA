Information ()
Definition: Information ABB is a Business Object describing business facts, assets, or opinions that are exchanged in the context of a digital public service to support its delivery. Information is enacted via interpretation rules (expressed in an ontology) of data and considering knowledge of a given context. Examples include an invoice, a medical prescription, a driving license. Source: TOGAF guide about business capabilities, TOGAF definition of information Source reference: (https://www2.opengroup.org/ogsys/catalog/g161)), (http://pubs.opengroup.org/architecture/togaf9-doc/arch/) Synonym: Business Facts, Business Data, Business Opinions Example: The following implementation is an example on how this specific Architecture Building Block (ABB) can be instantiated as a Solution Building Block (SBB): Surveillance3 - Surveillance information: Published Surveillance information (Business Information) on Europa: Textile category 1 - Cotton yarn, not put up for retail sale [Council Regulation (EEC) No 3030/93] from Belarus in 2015: see URL for details(http://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/dds2/surv/surv_data_list.jsp?Lang=en&survNumber=670010&survType=1&startDate=20150101&originCode=BY) LOST view: Organisational view Interoperability saliency: The Information ABB is salient for organisational interoperability because it represents the data being exchanged between organisations. Its interoperability needs to be guaranteed using organisational and semantic interoperability specifications.
