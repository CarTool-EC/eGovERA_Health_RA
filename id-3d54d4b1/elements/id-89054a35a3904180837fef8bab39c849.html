skos:definitionElectronic Healthcare Records (HHR) or Electronic Medical Records (EMR) is the service enabling the recording of medical events related to a patient through the entire patient journey. Electronic Medical Records (EMRs) are the digital equivalent of paper records, or charts at a clinician’s office. EMRs typically contain general information, such as treatments and medical history of a patient as it is collected by the individual medical practice and care outcomes.
