Patient summary system ()
Definition: The use or consumption of features provided by a Patient summary system enabling the management of patient summaries base registry at national level. Source: HealthIT.gov Source reference: https://www.healthit.gov/topic/health-it-basics/patient-summary Additional information: The Patient Summary System is a crucial tool in modern healthcare, enabling seamless communication between different healthcare providers and ensuring that all relevant information is available when needed. It can include a wide range of data, from basic demographic information to detailed medical histories. The system is typically designed to be easily accessible and user-friendly, allowing healthcare providers to quickly find the information they need. It can also include features such as alerts for potential drug interactions or allergies. By providing a comprehensive overview of a patient's health, the Patient Summary System can help healthcare providers make more informed decisions and provide better care. Example: A doctor in an emergency department can quickly access a patient's summary to understand their medical history and current medications, helping them make urgent treatment decisions. A specialist can use the Patient Summary System to review a referred patient's previous treatments and tests, reducing the need for duplicate procedures. A pharmacist can check a patient's summary for potential drug interactions or allergies before dispensing medication.
