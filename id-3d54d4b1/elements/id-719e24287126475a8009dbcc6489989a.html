dct:descriptionThe implementation of digital public services has a focus on integration, technology, governance or legal. Experience shows that a focused approach is a critical success factor in the implementation of digital public services. An assessment of the context is made prior to the implementation in order to determine the best focus.The priority of the implementation activities is determined by the chosen focus.Use mechanisms provided by the European Commission to plan and align the strategy with real needs. An example is IDPSIO, the IDPSIO (Interoperable Digital Public Services Implementation Orientation) Assessment Tool aims to support digital public service owners in deciding the approach to follow in order to implement their digital public services.""
