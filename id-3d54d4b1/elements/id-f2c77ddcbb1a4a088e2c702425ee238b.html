dct:description'A Declaration for the Future of the Internet' is a document that outlines the principles and values that should guide the development and use of the internet in the years to come. It emphasizes the importance of an open, accessible, and inclusive internet that respects and protects users' rights, promotes innovation, and fosters collaboration among stakeholders. The declaration calls for the protection of privacy, freedom of expression, and digital rights, while also addressing issues such as cybersecurity, digital literacy, and the ethical use of emerging technologies. It aims to provide a framework for shaping the future of the internet in a way that benefits individuals, societies, and the global community as a whole.
