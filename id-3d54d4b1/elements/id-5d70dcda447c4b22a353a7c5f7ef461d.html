skos:definitionTechnologies and devices that fall under the medical imaging umbrella include: radiography (X-rays), magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), computed tomography (CT), fluoroscopy, ultrasound, echocardiography, and nuclear medicine, such as PET (positron emission tomography). For diagnostic purposes, these devices/equipments are used to provide an image of the body and obtain a correct diagnosis while determining future care. Rapid advancements in technology and changes in health-care reform require a mindful approach in order to provide the correct system for the facility’s needs.
