dct:descriptionThe capability to digitally support a medical consultation. A medical consultation by definition is “a procedure whereby, upon request by one healthcare provider, another healthcare provider reviews a patient's medical history, examines the patient, and makes recommendations as to care and treatment.” Despite the definition, this business capability includes also processes and activities related to (digital) consultations directly asked/booked by people to a healthcare provider. Digital Consultation supports the remote booking of medical consultations and the execution of virtual medical consultations for monitoring and preventing diseases.
